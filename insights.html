Antibiotics

How clavulanate helps amoxicillin work

Many bacteria produce an enzyme called beta-lactamase that destroys amoxicillin. Clavulanic acid inhibits this enzyme, allowing amoxicillin to kill the bacteria effectively. This is why the combination is used where plain amoxicillin may fail due to resistance.

The nutrients that keep nerves healthy

Methylcobalamin, alpha lipoic acid, folic acid, thiamine and pyridoxine each play a different role: nerve regeneration, protection against oxidative stress, DNA repair and maintaining the myelin sheath that coats nerves.
