Export-Ready Pharmaceutical Formulations

In addition to our domestic portfolio, Mellorate Biotech is expanding its international presence with export-ready pharmaceutical formulations, helping make quality medicines more accessible globally.

Broad-Spectrum Antibiotic

Co-Amoxiclav Tablets

Co-Amoxiclav Tablets combine Amoxicillin with Clavulanic Acid to provide broad-spectrum antibacterial coverage against a wide range of susceptible bacterial infections. Manufactured under stringent quality standards, our Clavulanic Acid helps protect Amoxicillin from beta-lactamase-producing bacteria, enhancing its effectiveness.

Advanced Antibacterial Therapy

Cefuroxime + Clavulanic Acid Tablets

Cefuroxime + Clavulanic Acid tablets combine a second-generation cephalosporin antibiotic with a beta-lactamase inhibitor to support the treatment of susceptible bacterial infections. Developed with a focus on quality, safety, and consistency, this formulation is manufactured to meet pharmaceutical standards.

Quality Formulation for Global Healthcare

Cefuroxime Axetil + Clavulanic Acid Tablets

Cefuroxime Axetil + Clavulanic Acid Tablets are formulated to deliver reliable antibacterial therapy through the combination of an oral cephalosporin antibiotic and a beta-lactamase inhibitor. This product is manufactured in compliance with stringent quality control processes.
