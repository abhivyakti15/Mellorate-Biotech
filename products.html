Forever Gold

Advanced Neuro & Wellness Formula

Supporting Nerve Health. Enhancing Everyday Vitality.

A scientifically formulated nutritional supplement designed to support nerve health, energy metabolism, and overall well-being. Combining neurotropic vitamins with a powerful antioxidant, it is developed to help address nutritional deficiencies while promoting healthy nerve function and cellular health.

The formulation contains Methylcobalamin, the active form of Vitamin B12, along with Alpha Lipoic Acid, Biotin, Folic Acid, Thiamine (Vitamin B1), and Pyridoxine (Vitamin B6). Together, these ingredients provide comprehensive nutritional support for individuals requiring enhanced neurological care and metabolic health.

Key Component Benefits

Methylcobalamin
The active form of Vitamin B12, essential for nerve regeneration and treating peripheral neuropathy.
Alpha Lipoic Acid
A potent antioxidant that helps reduce oxidative stress in nerve cells and improves blood flow to the nerves.
Folic Acid
Supports cellular energy production and DNA repair.
Thiamine & Pyridoxine
B1 and B6 vitamins that are critical for maintaining the myelin sheath (nerve coating) and converting nutrients into energy.

Melomox 1000

Effective Antibiotic

Reliable Protection against Susceptible Infections

A high-quality combination of amoxicillin & clavulanate, developed to provide effective treatment against a wide range of susceptible bacterial infections. Manufactured to stringent pharmaceutical quality standards, it offers healthcare professionals a trusted antibiotic option for managing common bacterial illnesses.

Core Benefits

Overcoming Bacterial Resistance
The primary benefit of this combination is the addition of clavulanic acid. Many bacteria produce an enzyme called beta-lactamase that destroys amoxicillin. Clavulanate “inhibits” this enzyme, allowing the amoxicillin to effectively kill the bacteria.
Broad-Spectrum Efficacy
This high-dose formulation (875/125) is highly effective against a wide range of both Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria, making it a versatile “first-line” treatment for severe or persistent infections.

Specific Clinical Applications

Severe Respiratory Infections
A leading treatment for community-acquired pneumonia, acute bacterial sinusitis, and flare-ups of chronic bronchitis.
Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs)
Highly effective for complicated UTIs and kidney infections (pyelonephritis) where standard antibiotics might fail due to resistance.
Skin and Soft Tissue Infections
Frequently prescribed for cellulitis, infected animal bites, and severe dental abscesses.
Bone and Joint Infections
Due to its high dosage, it achieves good penetration for treating infections like osteomyelitis.

Antibiotics should be used only under medical supervision.
